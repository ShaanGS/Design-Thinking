Safety design: the AI does the talking, but a fixed rule list decides emergencies. Words like chest pain, sweating, breathlessness, a fall or slurred speech always escalate, whatever the AI thinks. Saathi never diagnoses or suggests new medicines.

The plastic bag of prescriptions becomes a medical passport in 20 seconds.

Families photograph old prescriptions from different doctors. Saathi reads them, builds one medicine list, and catches what no single doctor saw: duplicate brands and risky combinations.
