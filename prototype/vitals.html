Saathi Face Pulse

Heart rate from a 20-second selfie, with no device to wear.

Group 4 prototype · wellness check, not a medical device

Fit your face inside the oval. Good light on your face, sit still.

  • Face a window or a lamp; avoid light from behind.
  • Don't talk or move for 20 seconds.
  • For the demo, have a volunteer wear a pulse oximeter and compare the two numbers live.
How it works: each heartbeat slightly changes the colour of your skin. Saathi tracks that change in the forehead and cheeks (the POS method, Wang et al. 2017) and finds its rhythm. Studies show phone cameras measure heart rate closely; breathing rate and oxygen are less reliable, so Saathi only reports heart rate.
