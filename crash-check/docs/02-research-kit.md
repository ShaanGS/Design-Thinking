# Crash Check: Research Kit

Everything needed to run the Empathize and Test stages. Ask about their day and their last close call, not about our product, until the last section.

## Consent (read out before every interview)

> We are students from SRM working on a design project about road safety for riders. We'd like to ask you about your travel and your experiences on the road. It will take about 15 minutes. You can skip any question or stop at any time. With your permission we'll take notes [and record audio]. We won't use your name in anything we show. Is that okay?

Write down: date, place, age range, rides what, consent given (yes / audio yes).

## 1. Riders (students and working riders), 15 min

1. Walk me through your ride yesterday, from leaving to arriving.
2. Where is your phone while you ride? Has it ever fallen? What happened?
3. Tell me about the last time something went wrong on the road, or nearly did.
4. If you came off your scooter and couldn't move, who would find out first? How?
5. Who knows where you are when you ride? How do you feel about that?
6. Has anyone in your family asked to see your location? What did you do?
7. What would your parents do if they got a call saying you'd had an accident?
8. *(Only now, show the "Are you okay?" screen.)* What would you do if this appeared? What if it appeared when you'd only dropped your phone?
9. *(Show "What leaves your phone".)* In your own words, when can your mother see where you are?
10. Would you leave this on? What would make you turn it off?

## 2. Guardians (parents), 15 min

1. How do you know your son or daughter has reached safely?
2. What goes through your mind if they're late and not answering?
3. Have you ever heard about an accident (theirs or someone else's) late, or from a stranger? Tell me about it.
4. Would you want to see their location all the time? Why or why not?
5. *(Show Amma's alert screen.)* What do you do now? What would you press first?
6. Is there anything here you don't understand?

## 3. Bike-taxi and delivery riders, 15 min

1. How many hours a day do you ride? Where's your phone?
2. How often does your phone fall, or take a hard knock?
3. Has your company's app ever asked if you're okay? Was it right?
4. If an app asked "Are you okay?" after every pothole, what would you do?
5. Who should be told if you crash: family, the company, both?

## 4. 108 EMT or ER nurse, 15 min

1. When a road-accident call comes in, what information is usually missing?
2. How often is the location vague? What does that cost you?
3. What facts about the patient change what you do in the first ten minutes?
4. If the patient's phone showed blood group, allergies and an emergency contact on the lock screen, would you look? Would you trust it?
5. Have families ever arrived before the patient was identified? What happened?

## 5. Usability test sheet (prototype)

| Task | Say | Watch for | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | "You've just come off the scooter and you're fine. Make it stop." *(after 20 jumping jacks)* | Seconds to tap "I'm okay"; wrong taps | __ s |
| T2 | "You're hurt. Get help as fast as you can." | Finds "Get help now" | ✔ / ✘ |
| T3 | "Who can see where you are right now?" *(on the ride screen)* | Says "nobody" | ✔ / ✘ |
| T4 | "Your mother is at home. Show me what she sees after a crash." | Understands the one-hour limit | ✔ / ✘ |
| T5 | *(Guardian)* "This just rang. What do you do?" | First action and seconds | __ s |
| T6 | "Stop sharing your location." | Finds "I'm safe, stop sharing" | ✔ / ✘ |

Ask after: "What would make you trust this more? Less?"

## 6. Survey (Google Form, 2 minutes)

1. Age; what you ride; how many days a week.
2. Where is your phone while you ride? (mount / pocket / bag / other)
3. In the last month, how often did your phone fall or take a hard knock? (never / 1 to 2 / 3 to 5 / more)
4. Have you had a crash or fall on the road in the last year? (yes / no / prefer not to say)
5. Does anyone in your family see your live location? (yes / no / I turned it off)
6. "If I crash and don't answer my phone within 30 seconds, my chosen people should get my location for one hour." (1 to 5, disagree to agree)
7. "I would turn this off if it ever alerted my family by mistake." (1 to 5)
8. Would you leave Crash Check on? (yes / maybe / no) Why?
